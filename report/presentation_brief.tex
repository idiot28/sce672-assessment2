\section*{2. The Generated Cubic Polynomial Formula}
\vspace{-0.10cm}
Under differential flatness for unicycle kinematics ($\dot{x} = v\cos\theta, \dot{y} = v\sin\theta, \dot{\theta} = \omega$), the 2D position $\mathbf{z}(t) = [x(t), y(t)]^T$ is a flat output. Each flat coordinate is parameterized as a smooth $C^2$ cubic polynomial over mission execution horizon $T = 13.00\text{~s}$:
\begin{equation}
    z_j(t) = \alpha_0^{[j]} + \alpha_1^{[j]}t + \alpha_2^{[j]}t^2 + \alpha_3^{[j]}t^3, \qquad j \in \{x, y\}
\end{equation}
The boundary conditions for Tag $T_3$ ($d_0 = 1.50\text{~m}$, target standoff $x_f = 1.30\text{~m}$, crawl start $\dot{x}_0 = 0.06\text{~m/s}$, halt $\dot{x}_f = 0.0\text{~m/s}$) define the linear boundary value system $\mathbf{M}(T)\boldsymbol{\alpha} = \mathbf{b}$:
\begin{equation}
    \begin{bmatrix}
        1 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
        0 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
        1 & T & T^2 & T^3 \\
        0 & 1 & 2T & 3T^2
    \end{bmatrix}
    \begin{bmatrix}
        \alpha_0^{[x]} \\ \alpha_1^{[x]} \\ \alpha_2^{[x]} \\ \alpha_3^{[x]}
    \end{bmatrix}
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        x(0) \\ \dot{x}(0) \\ x(T) \\ \dot{x}(T)
    \end{bmatrix}
    =
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0.000 \\ 0.060 \\ 1.300 \\ 0.000
    \end{bmatrix}, \qquad
    \mathbf{b}_y = 
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0.000 \\ 0.000 \\ 0.000 \\ 0.000
    \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}
Inverting $\mathbf{M}(13.0)$ yields the solved polynomial coefficient vector $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_x = [0.000000, 0.060000, 0.013846, -0.0008284]^T$. The resulting closed-form analytical equations generated and executed in code (\href{https://github.com/idiot28/sce672-assessment2/blob/main/src/flatness_math.py}{\texttt{src/flatness\_math.py}}) are:
\begin{align}
    \boxed{x_{\text{ref}}(t) = 0.060000\,t + 0.013846\,t^2 - 0.0008284\,t^3 \quad (\text{m})} \label{eq:poly_x} \\
    \boxed{v_{\text{ref}}(t) = \dot{x}_{\text{ref}}(t) = 0.060000 + 0.027692\,t - 0.002485\,t^2 \quad (\text{m/s})} \label{eq:poly_v} \\
    \boxed{a_{\text{ref}}(t) = \ddot{x}_{\text{ref}}(t) = 0.027692 - 0.004970\,t \quad (\text{m/s}^2)} \label{eq:poly_a} \\
    \boxed{y_{\text{ref}}(t) \equiv 0.000\text{~m}, \qquad \dot{y}_{\text{ref}}(t) \equiv 0.000\text{~m/s}, \qquad \omega_{\text{ref}}(t) \equiv 0.000\text{~rad/s}}
\end{align}
Peak velocity occurs at $t^* = \frac{0.027692}{2 \times 0.002485} = 5.57\text{~s}$, reaching $v_{\max} = 0.137\text{~m/s}$, safely below the robot limit ($v_{\text{limit}} = 0.160\text{~m/s}$).

\vspace{-0.20cm}

\noindent\textbf{Key Analytical Comments:}
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep,topsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Dynamic Lag vs. Steady-State Precision:} Longitudinal error $e_x(t)$ peaks at $0.751\text{~m}$ during mid-run cruise due to the chassis acceleration filter and motor inertial lag. However, during the terminal deceleration phase ($t > 10\text{~s}$), $e_x(t)$ rapidly contracts, yielding a steady-state terminal position error of just $\mathbf{1.96\text{~cm}}$.
    \item \textbf{Nonholonomic Invariance:} Lateral error $e_y(t)$ remains bounded below $2.8\text{~cm}$ throughout the run and finishes at $0.4\text{~cm}$, validating that the commanded unicycle trajectory prevents chassis crabbing.
    \item \textbf{Pure-Vision Standoff Guard:} When the tag leaves the camera view at $d \le 0.35\text{~m}$, incremental wheel integration tracks remaining distance. The active brake engages at $d = 0.200\text{~m}$, bringing the chassis to a complete stop at $d = 0.220\text{~m}$ with zero external ToF sensor.
\end{itemize}
